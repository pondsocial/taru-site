Support

Something not working, or an idea to make Taru better? Write to privacy@pond.social and we'll get back to you. Testing through TestFlight? You can also take a screenshot in Taru and send it as feedback.

Common questions

How do I invite friends?
Create a club under Clubs and share its six-letter code, link or QR code. Friends enter the code under Clubs > Join with a code.

Why don't I get notifications?
Turn on the daily reminder in Profile and allow notifications when your iPhone asks. Notifications from club mates can be switched off under Profile > Club mates' reading.

How does the weekly leaderboard work?
Clubs rank by minutes read in reading sessions. Weeks start on Monday at 00:00 UTC.

How do I delete my account?
Profile > Delete account. Everything is removed; see the privacy policy for details.
