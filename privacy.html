Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Taru helps you read more, on your own and with friends. To do that it keeps your shelf, your reading sessions and your clubs. It doesn't show ads, doesn't track you across other apps or websites, and doesn't sell your data.

Who we are

Taru is made by Pond Platforms Oy, Finland, which is responsible for your data. Questions and requests: privacy@pond.social.

What stays on your phone

Your daily goal and reminder time are kept on your device. Photos you take of book covers are saved only on your iPhone and are never uploaded; they are deleted when you remove the book or your account. The apps you choose to block during reading use Apple's Screen Time and are stored only on your device as private tokens that even we can't read. They are never sent to us.

Who sees what

Only people in a club with you see your name, your weekly reading minutes, the books and minutes of your sessions, the notes you choose to share, and your kudos and replies. Notes marked as spoilers stay hidden until a club mate taps them. Notes you keep private, your book notes, your shelf and your reading path are visible only to you.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores Taru's data and runs its server functions. Where data is processed outside the EU or EEA, it's protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and delivers notifications. If you test Taru through TestFlight, Apple also collects crash reports and feedback you choose to send.
  • Open Library (Internet Archive) answers book searches. When you search or scan a barcode, the search text or ISBN is sent to Open Library, without your account or name.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, everything listed above is deleted from our database straight away. Copies may remain in our provider's backups for a short time before they expire.

Deleting your account

In Taru, open Profile and choose Delete account. This removes your profile, shelf, sessions, notes, paths, club memberships, kudos, replies and device tokens. Clubs left without members are deleted too. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple with Taru under Settings > your name > Sign-In & Security on your iPhone.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by writing to privacy@pond.social. We process your data to provide Taru to you, and to keep it secure and improve it. Notifications only arrive if you allow them on your phone. If you think we've handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland (tietosuoja.fi) or the authority where you live.

Age

Taru is for people aged 13 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
